How to read this

A low cut-off alerts on almost everything, so review cost dominates. A high cut-off alerts on almost nothing, so missed fraud dominates. The cheapest single cut-off sits in between, and it is usually far below 0.5 because a missed fraud costs much more than a review. On IEEE-CIS the amount-aware expected-loss rule beats every single cut-off because it uses a lower bar for larger transactions. On the small ULB set a few cut-offs come out slightly cheaper on test, within the noise of 89 frauds. Read the full reasoning in choosing a fraud threshold by cost.

Costs: missed fraud = amount + $15 chargeback, alert = $5 review. The curve is computed on the IEEE-CIS last month (days 152 to 182) and the ULB last 9 hours.

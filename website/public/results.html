Fraud detection results on real data

All numbers are on the final, held-out time period, using thresholds chosen on the period before it. Total cost = fraud dollars missed + $15 chargeback per missed fraud + $5 per alert reviewed.

The expected-loss rule saves 74% of the no-model cost and catches 83% of fraud dollars. It uses half the alerts of the best single threshold for a lower total cost, because it spends review time on the expensive transactions. The thresholds that maximise accuracy or F1 look better on paper and miss more than $320,000 of fraud.

What drives the IEEE-CIS model

The strongest features by LightGBM gain are customer-level aggregates (uid_C13_mean, uid_TransactionAmt_mean, uid_C13_std), Vesta's engineered features V257 and V258, the counting features C1, C13 and C14, how common the card is (card1_freq) and the device string. Customer behaviour over time matters more than any single field on the transaction.
